## Introdution

I recently designed and built a custom macropad from the ground up, and I’m excited to share the journey and details with you! This macropad is fully customizable and built to allow endless possibilities for expansion and creative use cases.

![Image of the final macropad product](/assets/images/macropad_image.png)

## The Design and Build Process

1. **PCB Design**: I designed the PCB using [KiCad](https://www.kicad.org/), ensuring a clean layout that maximizes the usability of the RP2040 microcontroller’s GPIO pins.
2. **Manufacturing**: The PCB was fabricated by [JLCPCB](https://jlcpcb.com/). They provided high-quality boards at an affordable price.
3. **Assembly**: I hand-assembled the macropad, carefully soldering each component and verifying connections.
4. **Firmware Development**: I wrote custom firmware for the RP2040 to interface with the macropad and ensure seamless functionality.

## Features and What Makes It Unique

Unlike other macropads, this one doesn’t stop at basic key functionality. All unused GPIO pins on the RP2040 are exposed, allowing for unparalleled expandability. Here are just a few ideas for what you can do:

- **Add a Screen**: Connect a small I2C or SPI display to show animations, system stats, or custom menus.
- **Attach a Knob**: Incorporate a rotary encoder for volume control, scrolling, or other interactions.
- **Connect Sensors**: Integrate temperature, light, or motion sensors for creative applications.
- **Integrate LEDs**: Add addressable RGB LEDs for vibrant lighting effects.
- **Build Custom Modules**: Create your own hardware extensions to use the GPIO pins in any way you can imagine.

The possibilities are endless—if it can connect to the RP2040’s GPIO pins, you can make it work with this macropad.
